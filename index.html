About

I'm a Business Intelligence Specialist at dentsu Global Services, based in India. My days are spent turning digital advertising data into client-facing reporting — Looker Studio and Power BI dashboards fed by Supermetrics and the ad platforms themselves, covering Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, DV360, CM360 and Google Analytics.

Before dentsu I spent just over two years at JLL building Tableau and Power BI reporting on top of PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Redshift and S3, and a year at FICO working alongside the product team — writing user stories in Jira by day and reporting on delivery progress by week.

The foundation underneath all of it is engineering: a BE in Information Technology from Mumbai University, an Oracle Java certification, and a first job writing Mulesoft and Java connectors for the Collibra data governance platform. An MBA in IT on top of that is what keeps me fluent on the business side of the table.
